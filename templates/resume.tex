\section*{Summary}
Two to four sentences: what you do, the tools you have really used (the keywords recruiters search for), your strongest concrete result, and availability (e.g. immediate joiner, open to relocation).

\section*{Skills}
\begin{itemize}[label={},leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Area:} Tool, tool, tool (only what you have used for real)
  \item \textbf{Learning:} Tools you are learning, kept apart so nobody mistakes them for experience
\end{itemize}

\section*{Experience}
\role{Job Title, Company}{Mon YYYY -- Mon YYYY}{One line of context: team size, your scope.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Verb first: what you built or ran, with which tools, and the result (numbers only if they are real).
  \item One idea per bullet, at most two lines.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Projects}
\project{Project name}{\href{https://github.com/you/project}{github.com/you/project}}
What it does and what you did, in one or two sentences.
