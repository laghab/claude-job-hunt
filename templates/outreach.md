# Outreach

## Templates
{first line} = one sentence specific to the recipient (their post, product, stack). Never reused.

**Cold email.** Subject: <Role> engineer, quick question about {company}
> Hi {name or team}, {first line}. I'm <name>. <one concrete thing you did, from the resume>. Do you have an opening now, or expect one soon? If someone else handles hiring, I'd be grateful for the right contact. Resume and site: <site>. Thanks, <name>

**Reply to a recruiter's post.** Subject: {role as written in the post} - <name>. Resume attached.
> Hi {name}, I saw your post about the {role} opening{ at company}. {first line}. <one concrete thing you did>. I can join <when>. My resume is attached. Thanks, <name>

**Follow-up 1 (day 4, same thread)**
> Hi {name}, following up on my note from {weekday} about {role} roles at {company}. Happy to share anything else that would help. Thanks, <name>

**Follow-up 2 (day 9, same thread, last one)**
> Hi {name}, one last note from me. If there's nothing open now, I'd be glad to be kept in mind for {role} roles later. Thanks for your time, <name>
